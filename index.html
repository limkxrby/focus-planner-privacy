Information you enter and how the app uses it

The app stores planner records and settings in its private app storage on your device. These can include tasks, notes and steps; recurring tasks; calendar event titles, descriptions, optional location text, dates, times, time zones and reminders; habit names and completion history; manual income and expense entries and savings goals; meal ideas, ingredients, plans and groceries; module/theme and low-energy preferences; focus-timer state; and reminder delivery history. The starter workspace is clearly labelled demonstration data. The information you enter can be personal or sensitive even when the app does not ask for your legal identity.

The app uses these records on the device to show schedules and progress, calculate manual budget balances and savings, generate grocery suggestions, run the timer and display reminders while the app is visible. Event location is text you supply; the app does not ask for device location. The app does not require or create an account.

Network and developer services

The app bundles its interface and supports offline use. Its application code contains no endpoint that automatically uploads planner records and no developer-operated sync, analytics, ads, crash reporting, subscriptions or payment feature. The release requests Android Internet permission for its WebView/Capacitor environment; the permission itself does not establish a transfer. Android, Google Play, the device maker and an external file/share destination may process data under their own terms. A complete on-device network trace across devices and providers has not been recorded. We will update this policy if the app adds SDKs or changes its network behavior.

Copies you choose to create or share

When you ask, the app creates a JSON backup containing the planner workspace or an ICS calendar copy containing selected event details and scheduled tasks. It opens Android's document picker or share sheet and passes the copy to the destination you select. That destination may save or transmit the file, including through a cloud account. The app does not select a cloud recipient for you. Sharing a calendar makes a copy, not a live synchronized calendar or collaborative account.

JSON and ICS files are readable and have no extra app-provided encryption. They can contain private notes, event locations and financial details. Review the destination and protect copies. A calendar share can leave a temporary file in the app's private cache; Android may clear that cache, but the app promises no automatic deletion time.

The app reads a JSON backup only when you select it. It validates the file and asks you to confirm before replacing the current planner. Unsupported or malformed imports leave existing records unchanged. Replacing records intentionally removes the prior local workspace unless you kept a separate copy.

Storage, retention and deletion

Local records remain until you edit/delete them, replace them with an imported backup, clear the app's storage or uninstall. Some individual records have delete controls; to erase the whole local workspace, use Android Settings > Apps > Focus Planner > Storage > Clear storage. This also clears local preferences and private cache. Export a backup first if you want to retain information.

The app sets Android allowBackup=false and excludes its app root from Android 12+ cloud backup and device-transfer rules. The effect of any independent manufacturer migration tool has not been verified on every device. Saved or shared JSON/ICS files are separate copies. Clear storage or uninstall does not delete them from Downloads, removable drives, another app or a cloud provider; delete each copy where it was saved. The publisher cannot remotely retrieve or delete records that exist only on your device.

Security and support

Android app-storage protections and your device lock help protect local records. The app does not add a separate password or encryption layer to its local database or exported files. Keep your device and exports secure. No storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

If you contact support, the publisher may receive the email address, message, files and other information you choose to send outside the app. Only the owner accesses the support mailbox. Messages remain until manually deleted; no fixed retention period applies. Contact us at limm.kirby@gmail.com to request deletion of your support correspondence, subject to applicable legal requirements. Do not send planner backups unless support specifically requires them and you choose to share them.

Audience and policy changes

Focus Planner is intended for adults aged 18 and older. The app has no account, public chat or social feed. If the app or its data practices change, the publisher will update this policy and its effective date and provide notice or consent where required.
